Dates with year: \b[A-Z][a-z]+\s+\d{1,2},\s+\d{4}\b
Phones: \b\d{3}-\d{3}-\d{4}\b
Comma-grouped numbers: \b\d{1,3}(?:,\d{3})+\b
Capitalized words: \b[A-Z][a-z]+\b
Standalone whole numbers: (?<![\w,\-])\d+(?![\w,\-])
NOTE: Capitalized words include sentences/months, not just personal names.
NOTE: Standalone-number pattern depends on what counts as a whole number in context.
